# Brief — AI strategy · assist ≠ automate

*Source: `Strategie_IA_COMEX_Indigo.pptx` (slide 3 + notes; title from slide 1) → `source.md` ·
Language: FR for the talk (EN source kept) · Target length: ≈ 30 s · 3 acts · 9 beats*

## Audience
Indigo's executive committee (COMEX), October 2026. Shown full screen as the **opener of the talk, before slide 2**;
the speaker then explains. So: a title at the start, no call to action at the end, keywords only.

## The one idea
"AI" covers two different jobs: **assisting** (a person stays in charge) and **automating** (the system decides
alone) — they are not built the same way.

### Beats (draft for G2)
1. `title` — AI STRATEGY · BEYOND THE MYTH (title card).
2. `two_jobs` — the title's "AI" becomes the chip; two rails: ASSIST · SLOW (left), AUTOMATE · FAST (right).
3. `ask` — the CRC agent's chat (badge); a customer request arrives; CUSTOMER HISTORY lights up.
4. `suggest` — the chip drafts a SUGGESTED REPLY into the chat (keywords: DRAFT).
5. `validate` — the agent reviews: ✓ VALIDATED; captions HUMAN IN THE LOOP · ERROR CAUGHT.
6. `tickets` — a stream of tickets on rails towards a ROUTER module (RULES · MODELS).
7. `route` — tickets leave fast towards AGENT A / B / C; captions NO HUMAN · BOUNDED ERROR.
8. `uncertain` — one ticket "?" is sent up to a person (icon): UNCERTAIN → HUMAN.
9. `words` — the two halves condense into ASSIST ≠ AUTOMATE.

## Actors (all from the kit)
- **Chip** — the AI ("AI" / "IA"), centre; the word of the title becomes it.
- **Chat + badge** — the CRC agent (assist side). Suggested reply = a chat reply (draft lines → text), ✓ = validation.
- **Data block** — CUSTOMER HISTORY.
- **Rails + packets** — requests and tickets travel along right-angle rails.
- **Module** — the ROUTER (icon: list / scope), with the label RULES · MODELS.
- **Panels / pills** — the three agent queues AGENT A · B · C.
- **User icon** — the person who receives the uncertain ticket.
- Colours: assist = **cyan** (meaning, human-paced); automate = **orange** (control, bounded); uncertain = red → back to
  ice (human); validated = green.
